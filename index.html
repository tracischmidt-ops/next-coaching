Welcome, and thank you

Why we started Treia

We met through LinkedIn for business. Over two years, our standing weekly conversations became a friendship built on trust and honest encouragement. We talk about family, health, money, goals and what inspires us, because life doesn’t keep those things separate.

We’re not involved in each other’s daily decisions, and that distance lets us offer perspective we’d never find on our own: close enough to care, far enough to be honest. Treia is our way of sharing what we discovered, whether you reflect on your own or alongside a peer.

The Five Rings

Treia helps people bring fresh perspective and greater balance to their lives, independently or alongside a peer. It is built around five interconnected areas of life we call the Five Rings.

Life doesn’t separate the Rings. Health affects relationships, finances affect peace of mind, and curiosity creates opportunities. That’s why they overlap. In the Reflection you’ll look at each Ring on its own, then at how they fit together.
