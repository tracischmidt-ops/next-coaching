Welcome to the Balance Survey

This program is designed to help you reflect on five important areas of your life:

Health  •  Joy  •  Love  •  Wealth  •  Curiosity

Together, these five areas offer a simple way to see where you feel strong, where you may want more attention, and what greater balance could look like for you.

You are part of a small group of friends and family helping us test the Rings experience before we share it more broadly. We are testing both the content and the experience, so your honest feedback is extremely valuable.

What you will do
1

Discover Your Balance Rings

Reflect on each of the five areas and receive a picture of your current balance.

2

Help Us Improve

Tell us what felt clear, useful, confusing, or incomplete. Your feedback shapes the final experience.

3

Find Your Accountability Partner

Help us test how Balance could connect people with compatible accountability partners who encourage one another throughout the year.

Don’t worry — we won’t be making any official matches with this information, just testing to see if the survey gives us enough data to make sound matches.

There are no right or wrong answers. Simply respond based on where you are in your life today.

Thank you for helping us shape Balance. Your thoughtful and honest feedback will help us create an experience that feels useful, encouraging, and easy to navigate.

⏱ Approximately 15 minutes   ·   One link guides you through all three steps
